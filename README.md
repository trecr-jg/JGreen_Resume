## The problem I solve

Founders carry two full-time jobs: building the product and pitching for the next round of funding. While their heads are down, day-to-day operations quietly drift. Cash, runway, customer commitments and delivery risks end up scattered across spreadsheets, inboxes and memory.

I saw this firsthand at **Maxwell Data**, an early-stage B2B data startup. The founder brought me in to build the operating backbone: a runway model, customer pilot tracking and delivery health monitoring. The result is a live management system the founder uses to run the business.

**That is what I do.** I give founders a clear, honest view of their business, so they can stay focused on product and investors, knowing operations are in good hands.
